Privacy Policy

Fidelitica Utilities (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our") establishes this Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") regarding the handling of users' privacy information in the application "Notencil" (hereinafter referred to as the "Application") and on our official website.

Basic Principles

  1. The Application requests access to device storage; however, this permission is used solely for file management purposes. Your files will never be transmitted to external servers or collected without your explicit permission.
  2. All editing features of the Application run locally on your device. Content and information are never uploaded for cloud processing, artificial intelligence (AI) training, or AI utilization.

Storage of User Data

The Application is designed with user privacy protection as its core principle.

  1. All notes, handwritten strokes, imported PDF documents, and related content created or edited within the Application are encapsulated within a single file and saved exclusively in the directory selected by the user.
  2. Users may save or upload files to third-party cloud storage services (such as OneDrive, Google Drive, etc.); however, users bear full responsibility for the management and synchronization of any uploaded data.
  3. User files and data are never automatically transmitted to or stored on external servers managed by us.

Information Collection and Purposes of Use

The Application and our official website do not collect personally identifiable information, except for the limited circumstances outlined below:
  1. Diagnostic and Crash Data (Platform Provided)
    2. To improve application quality and fix defects, we may access aggregated, anonymous crash logs and usage statistics standardly provided by distribution platforms (such as the Microsoft Store, Google Play, etc.). These metrics contain no personally identifiable information. Consent for collection and data handling are managed properly in accordance with the terms and policies of each respective platform.
  2. Support and Inquiries
    3. When a user contacts us with inquiries or feedback via email, GitHub, or other channels, we use the information provided (such as email addresses, account usernames, or message contents) solely to respond and provide support.
  3. Legal Requirements
    4. We may disclose the limited information in our possession only when formal legal requests or orders are issued by authorized public bodies (such as courts or law enforcement agencies) pursuant to due process, and only to the minimum extent strictly required by law.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Policy may be revised without prior notice due to feature updates, changes in applicable laws, or operational requirements. If significant changes are made, notifications will be posted on this website or within the Application.

Governing Law

The interpretation and application of this Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Japan.
